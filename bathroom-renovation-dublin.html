Bathroom Service

Bathrooms built for comfort, durability, and everyday use

A successful bathroom renovation needs more than good-looking finishes. We focus on the practical details too, including plumbing, waterproofing, drainage, and clean installation so the space performs properly for years to come.

What is included in a bathroom renovation?

Bathroom projects are tailored to the room, budget, and layout, but these are the most common stages.

Plumbing and pipework

We coordinate the plumbing side of the renovation so toilets, basins, showers, and baths are installed correctly and work reliably.

Waterproofing

Wet areas need proper preparation before the finishes go on. We make sure the bathroom is ready for long-term performance.

Tiling and surfaces

Wall tiling, floor tiling, trims, and grout lines are finished with care to give the room a clean, durable look.

Showers and fittings

From shower enclosures to taps, sanitaryware, and accessories, we install the details that complete the finished bathroom.

Why choose us for your bathroom renovation in Dublin?

Bathrooms need accurate fitting and strong preparation behind the scenes, not just a nice finish on the surface. We deliver tidy work, reliable communication, and careful installation from demolition through to final fixtures.

  • Waterproofing and tiling handled with care
  • Clean installation of showers, screens, and sanitaryware
  • Practical advice on layout, fittings, and maintenance
