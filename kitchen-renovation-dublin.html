What is included in a kitchen renovation?

Every project is tailored, but most kitchen refurbishments include the core work below.

Layout and design

We help plan a layout that improves workflow, storage, and lighting so your kitchen feels better to use every day.

Cabinetry and worktops

Base units, wall units, panels, and worktops are fitted carefully for a clean finish and long-term durability.

Electrics and lighting

We coordinate kitchen electrics, sockets, appliance connections, and lighting upgrades as part of the overall fit-out.

Tiling and finishing

Splashbacks, floor tiling, trims, and decorating details are completed so the whole room feels cohesive and finished.

Why choose us for your kitchen renovation in Dublin?

Kitchen projects involve more than just fitting units. They need careful sequencing, tidy workmanship, and clear communication across different stages of the job. We focus on getting the details right from first fix to final handover.

  • Clear planning before work starts
  • Reliable fitting for cabinets, worktops, and finishes
  • Experienced handling of electrics, tiling, and installation details
  • Respectful, tidy work in occupied homes
