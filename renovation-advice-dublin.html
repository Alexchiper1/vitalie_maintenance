Renovation Advice for Dublin Homeowners

Practical tips to help you prepare for kitchen, bathroom, flooring, painting and extension work before you ask for a quote.

How to prepare before asking for a quote

The more clearly you describe the project, the easier it is to get a useful first estimate.

Take clear photos

Photograph the full room, the main problem areas and anything that may affect access, plumbing, electrics or finish choices.

Share rough measurements

You do not need perfect drawings. Even basic room dimensions and wall lengths help narrow down labour and material needs.

Set a budget range

A realistic range helps decide whether to focus on a full renovation, a phased approach or selected upgrades first.

What to compare when reviewing quotes

Price matters, but scope and finish details matter just as much.

Scope of work

Check what is included: removal, prep, installation, finishing, clean-up and waste disposal should all be clear.

Materials and finish level

Make sure you are comparing like with like. Different finishes, fittings and prep standards can change the price significantly.

Timeline and access

Ask how long the work is expected to take, what order the stages will follow and whether you can still use parts of the home during the job.

FAQ

Common planning questions before renovation work starts.

Should I get quotes before choosing exact finishes?

Yes. A first quote can still be useful with a finish level in mind, even if the final tile, flooring or fittings are not selected yet.

What details help most when asking for a renovation quote?

Photos, rough measurements, your location, the type of work needed, target budget and preferred start timing are the most helpful starting points.

Can one team handle several trades together?

Yes. That is often the easiest way to coordinate bathrooms, kitchens and other projects where tiling, flooring, plumbing, painting and finishing overlap.
